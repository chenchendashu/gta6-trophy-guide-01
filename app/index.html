Leonida Platinum

Every one of the game's 55 trophies with its official name, description and grade, a guide to each in our own words that cites its sources, a roadmap to the platinum, and the missable trophies flagged — all saved in your browser, all working with the network off.

The official list: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. Sources: the official list on Exophase and PSNProfiles, named beside every guide.

Leonida Platinum is an unofficial, open-source guide (MIT licensed). Trophy names and descriptions are PlayStation's, shown as published; every guide is written for this entry and cites its sources. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshots are used — the look is this guide's own.
